## The marks

Every factual claim is marked **D** (a document says it), **R** (published reporting says it) or **[P]** (presumed: our inference from the pattern; no source states it). A claim that a named person did something wrong carries D or R, with a source that names them. It never rests on [P].

## The monitor

The board is checked by a monitor: Claude, Anthropic's AI, working on a schedule. It reads court dockets, agency releases, the Federal Register and news reporting, saves the documents, and drafts board entries with their marks and sources. Its last check is the time in the top line of every page.

## The review

Amanda reviews the board and decides what runs. The "Last reviewed by Amanda" stamp changes only when she signs off; scheduled updates never set it. If the monitor's time is newer than her review, the newest entries haven't had her eyes yet.

## The front page

Amanda picks the lead, writes the editor's note and signs it, by hand, once a week.

## The phrase trail

We track distinctive wording as it moves from think-tank plans into orders, contracts and court filings. A match in wording is how we find connections; what each match means is marked.

## Corrections

When we get something wrong, we strike it and log it, dated, in the [corrections log](/corrections/).
