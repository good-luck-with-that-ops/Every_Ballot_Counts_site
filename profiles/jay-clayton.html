Why we’re watching

  • As Director of National Intelligence he owes the President the assessment of foreign interference in the 2026 midterms, due 45 days after the election, about Dec 18. Nothing in the order requires a public version. {D}
  • On Nov 17 the White House chief of staff made him the administration’s voice on the month’s outages in place of the Homeland Security Secretary: “the decision was made to put Jay out there.” {R}
  • On Nov 22 he said the outages “were not coordinated and that they were not state sponsored.” Asked who breached the FBI and the Agriculture Department: “Again, not getting into specifics.” {R}
  • At his July confirmation hearing he would not say who won the 2020 election; a month earlier he said of election integrity, “we’re doing an absolutely terrible job.” {R}
  • He chairs the White House Super Intelligence Force. His career before April 2025 was in corporate law and finance; his Senate questionnaire lists no intelligence post. {R} {D}
  • For the two confirmed break-ins, the official who decides what counts as foreign interference has named no country and ruled none out. {P}
