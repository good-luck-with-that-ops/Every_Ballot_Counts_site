Why we’re watching

  • On Nov. 16 the president nominated him to be Comptroller General, the head of GAO, for a single 15-year term that no president can cut short. {R} {D}
  • Since November 2025 he has run USAID’s closeout, including an April 2026 plan to reserve $19.2 billion that 17 senators, writing to him by name, called an illegal impoundment. {D}
  • Since September 2025 he has performed the duties of OMB’s Controller, whose office prepares the government-wide financial statements GAO is required by law to audit. GAO’s latest audit faulted that preparation process. {D}
  • As OMB’s Deputy Director for Management he was the statutory Executive Chair of the inspectors general’s council while OMB withheld about $28 million of its funding. {D}
  • If he’s confirmed, the person who helped run that office would decide whether its actions were legal, whether to sue over them, and whether its books pass audit. {P}
