The Record / People map

The (Not Very) Deep State

This is the network around the people we’re watching: the officials who signed, the staff who built, the judges who ruled, the vendors who were paid, the lawyers and donors beside them, and the ordinary ties that connect them (old employers, law schools, boards). Being on this map is not an accusation. Start with the whole network, then pick anyone to open their file.

This map is a work in progress. Every line carries the source it came from, and we’re still checking them one by one.

    Filters

    Set the filters here, then move around the map freely. Tick as many as you like in each one: the map lights up everyone in any of them and rings in amber the people who are in all of it. Tick two or more people to see what they share.

    How to read the map

    Each dot is a person. The other shapes are the agencies, companies and think tanks that at least two of them have in common, and the bigger the shape, the more lines run to it.

    A solid line rests on a document and a dashed one on reporting. Hover over anyone to see who they’re connected to, and click to open their file. Opening a file doesn’t change your filters.

    marks a Person of Interest: someone Amanda has chosen to watch. is a Monitor Mark: the monitor adds it by count when a person keeps turning up in the record. Neither one says anyone broke a law.

    To zoom, pinch, hold Ctrl or ⌘ while you scroll, or use the buttons. Once you’ve zoomed in, drag to move around.
