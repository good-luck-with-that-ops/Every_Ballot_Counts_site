## Who's behind this

Every Ballot Counts is run by Amanda, a Boston-based lawyer who has spent about fifteen years negotiating data and technology contracts. It's an independent, personal project: no organization funds it and no employer endorses it.

Research help comes from readers who send tips and sources, and from Claude by Anthropic, which pulls and sorts documents and helps build the site. Amanda reviews what gets published and decides what runs.

## What it's for

Two jobs. First, catch what the government is setting up before it's reported, and put it on the [tripwire board](/tripwires/). Second, compile every source, documents and published reporting, so anyone can use them in their own research. The [timeline](/record/timeline/), the [registry](/record/registry/) and the [maps](/record/architecture/) are free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.
