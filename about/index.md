## Who's behind this

I'm Amanda. I'm a lawyer in Boston, and I've spent about fifteen years negotiating data and technology contracts. Every Ballot Counts is an independent, personal project. Nobody funds it and no employer endorses it.

I get research help from readers who send tips and sources, and from Claude (Anthropic's AI), which pulls and sorts documents and helps build the site. I review what gets published, and I decide what runs.

## What it's for

It has two jobs. The first is to catch what the government is setting up before it's reported, and put it on the [tripwire board](/tripwires/). The second is to compile every source (the documents and the published reporting) so anyone can use them in their own research. The [timeline](/record/timeline/), the [registry](/record/registry/) and the [maps](/record/architecture/) are free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

## How to read it

Every factual claim carries one of three marks. The "?" at the top of any page will remind you what they mean.
